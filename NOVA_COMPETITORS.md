# NOVA Competitors

## Research Caveat

This document summarizes current competitor research from public surfaces available during planning. Marketplace claims, provider counts, ratings, prices, and availability should be treated as unverified unless independently validated from official data, app stores, or live product testing.

NOVA must not claim to be the first product of its type without a dedicated market verification process.

## Tunisia Competitors

### Servicy.tn

Positioning: Tunisian home-service marketplace, close to an Uber-like model for artisans and repairs.

Observed strengths:

- Clear service booking promise
- Upfront price examples
- Cash after completion messaging
- Provider vetting claims
- In-app contact and tracking claims
- Local dialect-friendly positioning

Risks or weaknesses:

- Some public traction claims require verification
- Sample service/provider pages may show low review/completion evidence
- Trust claims need proof at transaction level

Implication for NOVA: Servicy is the strongest local reference for managed service booking. NOVA must exceed it through AI scoping, multilingual intent, task memory, and transparent execution history.

### Service.tn

Positioning: broad Tunisian service directory.

Observed strengths:

- Large category breadth
- Search and filter UX
- Claimed business listings across many sectors
- No-commission directory model

Risks or weaknesses:

- Directory is not the same as task execution
- Many listings may lack reviews
- User still compares, calls, negotiates, and follows up manually

Implication for NOVA: Service.tn shows supply discovery value. NOVA should not start by trying to replicate a national directory. It should own intent, planning, confirmation, tracking, and completion.

### Khedma / Kheddma

Positioning: lightweight local matching and request posting.

Observed strengths:

- Simple provider search
- Direct contact without heavy onboarding
- User can post requests with details and photos
- No commission messaging

Risks or weaknesses:

- Visible liquidity may be low
- More lead-generation than managed booking
- Limited execution accountability

Implication for NOVA: Request posting is useful, but NOVA should reduce user effort by structuring the request automatically and tracking outcomes.

### Maw3ed.tn

Positioning: broad appointment booking marketplace.

Observed strengths:

- Simple appointment concept
- Search, category, map, booking language
- Provider and client signup flows

Risks or weaknesses:

- Generic marketplace feel
- Some branding/template inconsistency observed
- Real provider depth and payments unclear

Implication for NOVA: Appointment booking is a future capability, not the MVP core unless real supply and availability exist.

### Serviplus

Positioning: multiservice app for individuals and businesses.

Observed strengths:

- Consumer and provider apps
- Provider subscription model reported around 20 TND/month
- No-commission positioning
- Provider validation messaging

Risks or weaknesses:

- Low visible app traction signals
- Security/data handling claims require verification
- Web surface appears limited

Implication for NOVA: Provider subscription is a potential future business model, but the MVP should validate user task completion first.

### Khademny / Khedamni

Research caveat: strongest visible match appears Algeria-oriented rather than clearly Tunisia-specific.

Implication for NOVA: Treat as an adjacent Maghreb benchmark, not a confirmed Tunisian direct competitor.

## International Competitors

### Thumbtack, Taskrabbit, Angi

Strengths:

- Mature local-service category UX
- Provider profiles, reviews, quotes, booking or lead flows
- Established marketplace trust patterns

Weaknesses:

- User still scopes work and compares options manually
- Incentives can optimize for paid leads rather than best outcome
- Limited persistent life-agent memory

NOVA implication: own the brief-to-outcome workflow, not only provider discovery.

### Uber and DoorDash

Strengths:

- Extremely low-friction execution
- Real-time tracking
- Upfront price or fee visibility
- Payments, support, ratings, receipts
- Habit loops and subscriptions

Weaknesses:

- Category-limited
- Can be expensive or fee-heavy
- Not a general planning assistant

NOVA implication: copy confidence UX patterns: status, ETA, cost, cancellation, receipt, support path.

### Amazon

Strengths:

- Search, comparison, reviews, checkout, delivery, returns
- Rufus and shopping assistant direction
- Strong retention through Prime and order history

Weaknesses:

- Optimized for Amazon ecosystem
- Sponsored discovery can reduce neutrality

NOVA implication: future shopping must be evidence-based and neutral. Do not invent prices or availability.

### Google, Perplexity, ChatGPT and AI Agents

Strengths:

- Strong natural language understanding
- Research, browsing, citations, connectors, memory, agent actions
- Fast improvement pace

Weaknesses:

- Broad, not locally operational
- Real-world fulfillment still fragmented
- Consequential actions need supervision

NOVA implication: generic AI can answer. NOVA must help users complete concrete life tasks with local context, permissions, auditability, and follow-up.

## White Space

The white space is a consumer life execution layer:

- Natural multilingual intent intake
- Structured task creation
- Controlled personal and household memory
- Provider/service discovery with real evidence
- Confirmation UX for medium and high-risk actions
- Task event history and follow-up
- Cross-marketplace orchestration over time
- Local execution depth before broad generic integrations

NOVA should not compete first on supply size. It should compete on making the path from need to outcome dramatically simpler.
